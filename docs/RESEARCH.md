# Couple Schedule - Market Research

## Problem Statement

Long-distance couples struggle to find mutual free time. Different timezones, conflicting work schedules, personal commitments (commuting, gym, errands), and the mental overhead of coordinating across calendars make it exhausting to plan quality time together. The question "when are you free?" becomes a timezone math problem.

## Competitor Analysis

### Direct Competitors (Couples-Focused)

#### 1. Cupla
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android
- **What it does:** Shared calendar designed specifically for couples. Syncs with Google, Outlook, and Apple calendars. Shows partner's schedule and timezone in one view.
- **Strengths:** Clean UI, timezone awareness, calendar import, free tier is generous
- **Weaknesses:** No automatic free-time detection/suggestion, no custom task blocking (commute, gym), no AI-powered scheduling, limited to calendar overlay -- still requires manual coordination
- **Pricing:** Free tier + Premium subscription

#### 2. TimeTree
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web
- **What it does:** Shared calendar with color coding per person, memo/chat per event
- **Strengths:** Mature, large user base (30M+), clean UX, shared + personal calendars
- **Weaknesses:** Not couples-specific, no timezone intelligence, no availability overlap detection, feels more like a family organiser

#### 3. Between
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android
- **What it does:** Private space for couples -- chat, photos, calendar, memos
- **Strengths:** All-in-one couples app, emotional features (anniversary tracking, photo sharing)
- **Weaknesses:** Calendar is basic, no external calendar sync, no timezone handling, no smart scheduling. More relationship scrapbook than productivity tool.

#### 4. Paired
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android
- **What it does:** Relationship wellness app -- quizzes, questions, games, basic calendar
- **Strengths:** Strong on emotional connection, good retention mechanics
- **Weaknesses:** Calendar is an afterthought, zero scheduling intelligence, no external integrations

#### 5. ClanPlan
- **Platforms:** iOS
- **What it does:** Shared calendar + location sharing + chat + to-dos
- **Strengths:** Modern UI, real-time location, place alerts
- **Weaknesses:** iOS only, more family-oriented, no timezone support, no smart availability matching

#### 6. OurCal
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android
- **What it does:** End-to-end encrypted shared calendar with external sync
- **Strengths:** Privacy-first (E2E encryption), calendar sync
- **Weaknesses:** Paid only, small user base, no availability detection

### Adjacent Competitors (Scheduling Tools)

- **Calendly** -- Meeting scheduling with timezone handling. Business-focused, not couples.
- **Reclaim.ai** -- AI calendar assistant, auto-scheduling. Work productivity, not relationship.
- **Fhynix** -- AI assistant for family scheduling. Family-focused, not couples-first.

## Market Gap

**No existing app combines all of these:**
1. External calendar integration (Google + Apple + Outlook)
2. Custom task/block insertion (commute, gym, meals, sleep)
3. Automatic free-time overlap detection across timezones
4. Smart suggestions for couple time (video call, movie night, game session)
5. Push notifications when new mutual free slots appear
6. Timezone-native design (not an afterthought)

**This is the gap.** Cupla comes closest but stops at calendar overlay -- it shows you the problem but doesn't solve it. Our app solves it.

## Target Audience

- Long-distance couples (different cities/countries/timezones)
- Couples with demanding/irregular schedules (shift workers, healthcare, tech)
- Age: 18-35 (primary), 35-50 (secondary)
- Tech-savvy, smartphone-native
- Willing to pay for relationship tools ($3-8/month)

## Monetization

**Recommended:** Freemium with generous free tier. Premium ($4.99/month or $39.99/year per couple) unlocks AI suggestions, unlimited calendar sources, advanced analytics.

## Key Insights

1. **Privacy is paramount** -- couples share intimate schedule data; E2E encryption is a differentiator
2. **Simplicity wins** -- Cupla and TimeTree succeed because they are simple. Don't over-feature.
3. **Timezone is underserved** -- most apps treat it as secondary; making it core is a major differentiator
4. **Emotional + functional** -- pure productivity apps feel cold for couples. Need warmth without being cheesy.
5. **Calendar fatigue is real** -- the app must reduce friction, not add another calendar to manage
