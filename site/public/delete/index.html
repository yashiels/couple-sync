Delete your account

Delete your account & data.

You can delete your Couple Sync account at any time. Deletion is permanent and immediate: it removes your account, every manual block either you or your partner created, your shared free time, your calendar connection, and your notification tokens, and it unpairs you both. Your Google Calendar itself is never touched — Couple Sync only ever read its free/busy times, and simply stops reading them.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Couple Sync and go to Settings.
  2. Under Couple, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted right away and you're signed out.

Don't have the app anymore?

If you've already removed the app, request deletion by emailing support@yashiel.dev with the subject line "Delete my account". So that no one can delete someone else's account, we verify you control that address before deleting — we'll reply to it to confirm. Requests are actioned within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

What is kept, and for how long

After deletion completes we keep only the account's internal identifier — a random string with no personal details attached — plus the deletion timestamps, so a deleted account cannot be silently recreated. Data held by Firebase (your sign-in identity and push token) is removed asynchronously shortly after. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.
