Delete your account

Delete your account & data.

You can delete your Couple Sync account at any time. Deletion is permanent and immediate: it removes your account, every manual block either you or your partner created, your shared free time, your calendar connection, and your notification tokens, and it unpairs you both. Your Google Calendar itself is never touched — Couple Sync only ever read its free/busy times, and simply stops reading them.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Couple Sync and go to Settings.
  2. Under Couple, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted right away and you're signed out.

Don't have the app anymore?

If you've already removed the app, request deletion by opening an issue on GitHub titled "Delete my account" and including the Google email address you signed in with. So that no one can delete someone else's account, we verify you control that email address before deleting — you'll be asked to confirm from it. Requests are actioned within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing of yours is retained after deletion completes. We keep only a minimal internal record that the account identifier was deleted, with no personal details, so a stale sign-in cannot silently recreate the account. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.
