Couple Sync

Find time together, across time zones.

Couple Sync connects to both partners' Google Calendars and shows the windows when you're both free — so the next call plans itself.

Get it on Google Play

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google. That one consent is also calendar access.
  2. Pair with your partner. Share a 6-character code.
  3. See your overlap. Free windows land on both phones instantly.

Your calendar stays yours

Couple Sync reads busy/free only — never an event title, never a guest list, never a location. Your partner sees when you're busy, not what you're doing. Read the privacy policy.

Have an invite code? Open it in the app, or see how pairing works.
