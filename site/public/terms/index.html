What Couple Sync is

Couple Sync is a free Android app that reads two partners' busy/free times and shows the windows when both are free. It's operated by an independent developer and is currently in beta. By using it, you agree to these terms and to the Privacy Policy.

Using it responsibly

  • You need a Google account, and you must be old enough to hold one in your country.
  • Connect only calendars you're entitled to, and pair only with someone who agrees to it.
  • Don't try to break, overload, reverse-engineer, or abuse the service or other people.

Your account and data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time — in the app under Settings → Delete account, or by requesting deletion. What we read, store, and never touch is spelled out in the Privacy Policy. Calendar access is free/busy only and revocable from your Google account at any time.

No warranty; limits

Couple Sync is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. It's a scheduling aid, not a source of truth — don't rely on it for anything critical, and always confirm plans directly.
